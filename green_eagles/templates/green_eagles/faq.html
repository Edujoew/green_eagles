Got questions about PLASTOUT, our operational wings, or clean-up events? Find answers below.

PLASTOUT is an umbrella organization based in Kericho County dedicated to combating plastic pollution, driving recycling and environmental education, and fostering youth leadership for sustainable climate action.

The Green Eagles Crew is an operational branch under PLASTOUT focused on scouting discipline, outdoor survival skills, patrol structures, and environmental stewardship in the field.

Messengers of Peace is a dedicated wing of PLASTOUT that leads community outreach, peace-building, conflict resolution, and social service projects alongside our environmental initiatives.

You can sign up through our Contact Page or speak directly with a Patrol Leader during our regular clean-up exercises and scouting sessions.

Collected plastics are sorted following our clean-up drives. Reusable items are repurposed, while recyclable plastics are processed through local recycling partners to ensure they stay out of Kericho's waterways and environment.
