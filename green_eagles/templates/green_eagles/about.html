About PLASTOUT

Uniting youth, community, and climate action for a plastic-free Kericho County.

Who We Are

PLASTOUT is an umbrella organization dedicated to environmental conservation, structural youth growth, and community empowerment across Kericho County.

We exist to fight plastic pollution at the source — through clean-ups, recycling, education, and active youth leadership. Under our umbrella, specialized wings carry out our mission on the ground.

Our Vision

To cultivate disciplined, active, and innovative leaders prepared to positively transform their communities and spearhead sustainable climate action.

Our Operational Wings

  • Green Eagles Crew:
    Focuses on outdoor survival, patrol structures, scout discipline, and environmental stewardship.
  • Messengers of Peace:
    Drives community outreach, conflict resolution, peace building, and humanitarian service initiatives.
