PLASTOUT KERICHO COUNTY  ·  PLASTIC-FREE SINCE THE FIRST CLEAN-UP

EVERY BAG WE PICK UP
IS ONE LESS IN THE RIVER.

PLASTOUT exists to fight plastic pollution at the source — through clean-ups, recycling, education, and the young people willing to do the work. Green Eagles Crew and Messengers of Peace are our boots on the ground.

Our Mission Get Involved
Collect & Recycle

Organized clean-up exercises across Kericho County, turning collected plastic into re-use and recycling opportunities instead of pollution.

Educate & Partner

Working with schools, government agencies and environmental organizations to teach communities the dangers of plastic pollution and build lasting solutions.

Lead & Serve

Growing teamwork, leadership and volunteerism among young people — through Green Eagles Crew and Messengers of Peace — for climate action that lasts.
