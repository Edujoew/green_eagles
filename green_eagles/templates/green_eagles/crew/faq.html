CREW KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining patrols, gear standards, advancement ranks, and field operations.

You can register through the main portal and select the Green Eagles Crew wing during signup. Once authenticated, your dashboard will assign you to an active local patrol group and link you with team commanders.

Standard gear includes proper hiking attire, a compass, notebook, water canteen, and designated patrol identification badges provided during orientation. Heavy operational equipment is checked out via the resource booking system.

Leadership roles such as Patrol Leader, Quartermaster, and Scribe are assigned based on active participation, demonstrated reliability during field training, and endorsement from senior coordinators.

Meeting schedules and muster points are updated weekly on the announcements board and your member dashboard. Special briefs are issued prior to any major competitions or outdoor excursions.

Proficiency badges earned through practical tests, camping exercises, and leadership tasks are recorded digitally. You can review your earned credentials directly from your profile dashboard.

For matters concerning travel coordination, competition logistics, or equipment checkouts, reach out to your patrol leader or submit an inquiry through the main contact portal.
